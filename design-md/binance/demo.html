Frequently Asked Questions

What is Binance and how does it work?
Binance is the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume. It provides a platform for buying, selling, and trading hundreds of cryptocurrencies with low fees, advanced trading tools, and industry-leading security.
How do I buy cryptocurrency on Binance?
You can buy crypto on Binance using a credit/debit card, bank transfer, or P2P trading. Simply create an account, complete identity verification, and choose your preferred payment method to start buying.
Is Binance safe and regulated?
Yes. Binance maintains the SAFU (Secure Asset Fund for Users) insurance fund, uses multi-tier and multi-cluster system architecture, and implements advanced risk controls. We are committed to regulatory compliance across all operating jurisdictions.
What are the trading fees on Binance?
Standard spot trading fees start at just 0.1% maker and taker. You can further reduce fees by holding BNB, increasing your 30-day trading volume, or becoming a VIP member.
How can I earn passive income with my crypto?
Binance offers multiple earning products including Simple Earn (flexible and locked staking), Launchpool, Liquid Swap, BNB Vault, and Dual Investment. Choose the product that matches your risk tolerance and earning goals.

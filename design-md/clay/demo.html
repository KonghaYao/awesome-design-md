FEATURES

Everything your GTM team needs to scale

AI-powered enrichment

Claygent auto-researches companies and contacts using AI — pulling from 50+ data providers to build complete profiles.

Waterfall enrichment

Chain data providers in sequence — if Provider A misses, fall through to Provider B, C, D. Maximize match rates automatically.

CRM enrichment

Sequencer & campaigns

Data tables & views

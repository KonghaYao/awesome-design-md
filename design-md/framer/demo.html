FAQ

Got questions?

Can I use my own domain with Framer?

Yes. Every Framer plan supports custom domains. You can connect your domain in one click from the site settings panel, and Framer automatically provisions SSL certificates.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Framer's canvas is visual — you drag, drop, and style everything on a freeform design surface. If you do know code, you can extend any component with custom React.

Can I export my Framer site?

Code export is available on Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans. You can download your site as a static React project and host it anywhere.

What's included in the CMS?

Framer's CMS includes collections, reference fields, filtering, conditional visibility, and webhooks. It's integrated directly into the canvas — manage content alongside your design.

Is there a free plan?

Framer offers a free starter plan that lets you build and preview sites. To publish with a custom domain, upgrade to any paid plan starting at $5/month per site.
